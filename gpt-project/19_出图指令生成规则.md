# 19_出图指令生成规则（新增）

## 1. 文件定位

本文件是整个系统中**离出图最近的一层**。

它的输入是：造型定义卡（来自 `12_概念方案输出模板.md`）  
它的输出是：可以直接粘贴给 DALL-E / Midjourney / 其他图像模型的出图指令

**为什么需要这份文件？**  
造型定义卡是给人看的——它用结构化字段描述设计意图。  
但图像模型读的是一段连续文字，它不理解字段名，不理解"母体定义"，更不理解"DNA"。  
这中间的转换如果做错，前面 13 步的工作全部作废。

本文件解决的问题：**如何把造型定义卡正确翻译成图像模型能执行的指令。**

---

## 2. 翻译的根本原则

### 原则 A：几何坐标语言，不是气质语言

图像模型理解的内容：
- 位置关系（在高度 60% 处 / 从左边缘延伸至右边缘）
- 尺寸关系（宽约 3mm / 占主体宽度的 85%）
- 几何形态（竖向圆台 / 连续曲面 / 横向细缝）
- 材质描述（磨砂半透白 / 深灰哑光 / 内透暗红光）
- 明确禁止（禁止出现 XX）

图像模型**不理解**的内容：
- "超跑感" → 必须翻译成"高度 35% 处轮廓向左右各外扩 8–10%"
- "机甲感" → 必须翻译成"主体表面有 4 条以上横向切线，底座有独立装甲片"
- "高级感" → 必须翻译成"表面磨砂哑光，无明显反光，分件线不超过 2 条"
- "一体化" → 必须翻译成"顶部、主体、底座为连续曲面，无台阶断裂"

### 原则 B：母体先于特征件，特征件先于 CMF

指令文字的组织顺序必须是：
```
整体形态 → 比例 → 体块过渡 → 特征件位置与形态 → CMF → 禁止项
```
不得颠倒（尤其不能把 CMF 写在前面，特征件写在后面）。

### 原则 C：禁止项必须写，且必须具体

如果不写禁止项，图像模型会默认填入训练数据里出现频率最高的元素。  
对于灯具类产品，图像模型的训练默认偏向以下元素：
- 顶部环形灯带（非横向细缝）
- 独立黑色顶盖
- 机甲风格底座
- 游戏外设配色（全黑 + 红色点缀）

**如果不明确禁止这些，它就会出现。**

### 原则 D：长度控制

出图指令推荐长度：150–300 词（英文）或 200–400 字（中文）。  
过短：缺少关键几何约束，模型自由发挥空间过大  
过长：模型会忽略后半段内容，重要禁止项可能被跳过

重要内容放在前 2/3，禁止项放在最后 1/3。

## 3. 出图指令标准结构

```
[产品类型 + 使用场景]
[母体几何：整体形态 + 高宽比 + 视觉重心]
[体块过渡：顶部/主体/底座如何衔接]
[特征件：位置 + 形态 + 尺寸 + 嵌入/附加]
[CMF：主体材质 + 底座材质 + 点缀色位置与表现方式]
[视角与背景：推荐视角 + 背景处理]
[禁止项：至少 3 条具体几何约束]
```

---

## 4. 逐段翻译规则

### 4.1 产品类型 + 使用场景

**目的：** 让模型建立基础产品认知，避免生成完全跑题的物体

**写法：**
> A portable camping lantern / desk lamp, product design render, studio lighting, white background

**禁止写法：**
> A futuristic device（太模糊，模型会自由发挥）  
> A supercar-inspired lamp（会直接生成跑车造型）

---

### 4.2 母体几何

**目的：** 锁定整体几何骨架，这是最重要的一段，必须排在最前

**写法模板：**
> The main body is a [几何类型], height-to-width ratio approximately [X:X]. The widest point is at [高度百分比] of the total height. Overall visual weight centered at [高度百分比].

**示例（竖向圆台）：**
> The main body is a tapered cylinder (wider at top, narrower at base), height-to-width ratio approximately 1.2:1. The widest point is at the upper third. Overall silhouette is vertically oriented with a stable, grounded feeling.

**示例（引入轻微横向张力）：**
> The main body is a tapered cylinder. At 35% of the total height, the body slightly expands laterally by about 8% on each side, creating a subtle shoulder-line tension. Height-to-width ratio approximately 1.1:1.

---

### 4.3 体块过渡

**目的：** 防止顶部/底座变成独立突兀的分件

**写法模板：**
> The top smoothly [transitions into / merges with] the main body as a continuous surface, no separate cap piece. The base [transitions / separates] from the main body with [a single thin seam line / two horizontal seam lines / a clear step].

**示例（连续曲面顶部）：**
> The top flows continuously into the main body as a single unbroken surface — no separate lid or cap. The base has one clean horizontal seam line separating it from the main body, approximately 2mm wide, but no independent protruding pieces.

**禁止写法（会产生突兀黑盖）：**
> Has a black top cover / dark lid on top（会生成独立黑盖）

---

### 4.4 特征件

**目的：** 精确定位记忆点，防止模型把特征件放错位置

**写法模板：**
> At [高度百分比] of the body height, there is [特征件描述], spanning [左右范围], approximately [宽度] wide, [flush with / recessed into / slightly protruding from] the surface.

**示例（横向发光细缝）：**
> At 62% of the body height, there is a single thin horizontal light slit, spanning the full width of the body from left edge to right edge, approximately 3mm wide, recessed slightly into the surface. The slit emits a dim dark red inner glow — not a bright beam, just a subtle warm-dark illumination.

**示例（多特征件时的数量控制）：**
> Only one primary feature element: the horizontal light slit described above. No other decorative features on the main body surface.

---

### 4.5 CMF

**写法模板：**
> Main body: [材质描述]. Base: [材质描述]. Accent: [颜色] at [位置], expressed as [内透光/表面涂装/金属件].

**示例：**
> Main body: frosted semi-translucent white, soft diffused surface with slight inner glow when lit. Base: dark gunmetal gray, matte finish with subtle horizontal texture, no metallic sheen. Accent: deep dark red, only at the horizontal light slit, inner glow only — no external halo effect.

---

### 4.6 视角与背景

**推荐写法：**
> 3/4 perspective view, product photography style, neutral dark gray gradient background, single key light from upper left, subtle reflection on base surface.

**注意：**  
- 不同视角对母体判断影响大，建议第 1 轮用 3/4 视角验证整体形态
- 正视图用于验证对称性和高宽比
- 不要用"dramatic lighting"——会产生过度反光破坏材质判断

---

### 4.7 禁止项

**这是出图指令中最容易被省略、但最重要的部分。**

**写法要求：**
- 每条禁止项必须具体描述"不允许出现什么几何/材质元素"
- 不允许写气质类禁止（如"不要机甲感"——模型不懂）
- 必须翻译成视觉元素（如"不允许底座出现突出于主体轮廓的独立装饰片"）

**标准禁止项模板（灯具类）：**

```
IMPORTANT — DO NOT include any of the following:
- No separate black top cap or lid that contrasts with the main body color
- No circular ring light at the top — the only light element is the horizontal slit at 62%
- No mechanical armor plates or protruding decorative pieces on the base
- No more than 2 horizontal seam lines on the entire product
- No gaming peripheral aesthetic — avoid aggressive black+red color scheme dominating the design
- No visible screws, bolts, or mechanical joint details
```

**每次出图必须至少写 4 条禁止项。**

## 5. 完整指令示例

以下是针对"超跑尾部DNA × 露营灯"案例的完整出图指令示例：

```
A portable camping lantern / desk lamp, industrial design product render, clean studio photography.

The main body is a tapered cylinder (wider at top, narrower at base), height-to-width ratio approximately 1.2:1. At 35% of total height, the body expands very slightly outward on both sides (about 8%), creating a subtle horizontal tension — the widest point of the product. The overall silhouette reads as vertical with a hint of lateral strength.

The top flows continuously into the main body as a single unbroken curved surface — no separate lid, no cap, no top piece. A small circular touch control is flush-embedded at the very top center (diameter approximately 25% of top face). The base separates from the main body with two clean horizontal seam lines, approximately 2mm each. The base has a subtle horizontal knurl texture but no independent protruding pieces of any kind.

At 62% of the body height, there is a single thin horizontal light slit, spanning the full width of the body from the left edge to the right edge, approximately 3–4mm wide, recessed slightly (about 1mm) into the surface. The slit emits a dim, warm dark-red inner glow — subtle, not a bright beam.

Main body: frosted semi-translucent white polypropylene, soft matte surface. When lit, interior light diffuses gently through the body. Base: dark gunmetal gray, matte finish, subtle horizontal texture. Accent: deep dark red, only at the horizontal slit, inner glow only.

3/4 perspective view, product photography, neutral dark background, single soft key light from upper left, subtle surface reflection on base.

IMPORTANT — DO NOT include any of the following:
- No separate black top cap, lid, or dark cover contrasting with the white body
- No circular ring light at the top or any circular light element — the only light element is the horizontal slit at 62% height
- No mechanical armor plates, fins, or protruding decorative pieces anywhere on the product
- No more than 2 horizontal seam lines total
- No gaming peripheral or mech aesthetic — no aggressive black+red color scheme dominating the product
- No visible screws, hinges, or mechanical assembly details
```

## 6. 多轮迭代中的指令调整规则

### 纠偏时
母体级纠偏：重写母体几何段（4.2）和体块过渡段（4.3），其余段可保留  
特征级纠偏：只重写特征件段（4.4），补充更多禁止项  
气质级纠偏：只重写 CMF 段（4.5），调整禁止项中的颜色约束

### 强化时
不重写母体段，只在以下位置调整：
- 高宽比数值微调（±0.1）
- 特征件深度/宽度增加（+1–2mm）
- CMF 对比度增强（更深的底座/更明确的点缀色）

### 收口时
只调整：视角、光线、背景描述，不改任何几何约束

## 7. 中英文指令选择

| 场景 | 推荐 |
|---|---|
| DALL-E 3 | 英文指令，模型对英文几何描述理解更稳定 |
| Midjourney | 英文指令 + --ar 参数控制比例 |
| 国内模型（如通义万象） | 中文可用，但几何描述部分建议保持精确数值 |

无论哪种模型，**禁止项必须用英文大写 IMPORTANT 标注**，提高模型对禁止内容的权重。

## 8. 常见失败模式与修复

| 失败表现 | 原因 | 修复方式 |
|---|---|---|
| 顶部出现黑色盖板 | 禁止项没写顶部盖板 | 加禁止项：No separate top cap or lid |
| 发光件变成顶圈灯带 | 发光件位置描述不够精确 | 加精确位置：at 62% height, horizontal slit only |
| 底座变成机甲装甲 | 底座描述过于简略 | 加描述：no protruding pieces, max 2 seam lines；加禁止项 |
| 整体变成游戏外设风 | CMF 描述中有"black"且无约束 | 改主色为 gunmetal gray / cold white，加禁止项排除 black+red dominant scheme |
| 每次生成都一样 | 指令过于封闭，留给模型的自由空间为零 | 放开一个次级 CMF 参数，让模型有轻微变化空间 |
| 高宽比不对 | 母体几何段没有写具体数值 | 加明确数值：height-to-width ratio approximately 1.2:1 |
